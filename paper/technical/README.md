# TGI: Topological Geometric Intelligence

The active publication manuscript is one article by Emylton Leunufna:

- [PDF](TGI_TOPOLOGICAL_GEOMETRIC_INTELLIGENCE.pdf)
- [Editable source](TGI_TOPOLOGICAL_GEOMETRIC_INTELLIGENCE.md)

The article integrates character identity and lattice addressing, band-owned
phase and organization, interventional spatial relations, deterministic
resolution with continual revision, and owned spatial execution. Its
bibliography cites the four NINMENI papers by author, year, full title, version
and SSRN record. Internal contracts and raw receipts are research artifacts,
not entries in the scientific bibliography.

The six manuscripts submitted separately on 4 October 2026 remain available
in the repository's prior Git revision `de95c4c` and the author's archive.
The consolidated article is a new document; it does not alter those submitted
PDFs or the review state at SSRN. The
[version map](POST_SUBMISSION_VERSION_MAP.md) separates the cited code
revision from subsequent package releases.

Render the editable source with `python tools/render_unified_paper.py`, then
validate it with `python tools/validate_unified_paper.py`. The local
publication manifest is in `validation.json`.
